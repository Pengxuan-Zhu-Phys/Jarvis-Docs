# Example: GM95 Excess

## Publication Note

`gm95excess` corresponds to the formal research work:
"Unified interpretation of 95 GeV di-photon and di-tau Excesses in the Georgi-Machacek Model"
by Qin Chang, Xiaokang Du (corresponding author), and Pengxuan Zhu.

- ArXiv: [2509.26155](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.26155)
- INSPIRE-HEP: [record](https://inspirehep.net/arxiv/2509.26155)
- Contact to us:
    - Xiaokang Du ([xkdu@hnas.ac.cn](mailto:xkdu@hnas.ac.cn));
    - Pengxuan Zhu([pengxuan.zhu@adelaide.edu.au](mailto:pengxuan.zhu@adelaide.edu.au)).
